Measure the available inside width and height, then enter your tube size and the centreline radius (CLR) of your actual bending die. Each member has its own CHS sizing. Harness bars connect the rear stays, optional continuous front legs follow the roof and A-pillars, and a windscreen header joins their upper sections. Supported tube-to-tube ends generate individually labelled 1:1 fishmouth wrap-arounds. Every enabled tube also has its own straight-blank marking diagram with bent-tube start/centre/end marks or straight-tube joint-centre reference stations, plus individual print and SVG export. All tube-to-tube joints · printable wrap-arounds

1:1 CHS developed OD · individual end labels

Each separate tube end is calculated against its actual 3D parent tube axis or the finished main-hoop centreline, including bends where a single cope contour is valid. Upper and lower ends, left and right sides, and harness ends are individually labelled. Rear stay footplates are not tube-to-tube joints.

Calculating joints…
—

Every sheet includes a 100 mm calibration line. Print at actual size / 100% with no fit-to-page scaling; measure the line before cutting. The reference UP/FRONT directions are defined for each tube end and may not match when the tube is reversed. Mark reference lines on the physical tube first. Templates represent ideal external-cylinder intersection without weld gap, kerf, tube deflection or coating. Treat the front end of a side bar as unverified until you enter and confirm the actual front/A-pillar tube OD and both lean angles. Use a test fit before cutting final cage components.

Straight-tube mark-out

All measurements from ONE datum end

Mark each bend three times: ● Start = where the curve begins; ● Centre = halfway through the curved length; ● End = where the curve finishes. Use the same tube end for every tape measurement. Centre means arc midpoint, not the intersection of the straight legs.

All marks include the optional extra length at the beginning of the blank. This is a geometric layout; follow your bender's own reference-mark method and compensate for actual bend growth and springback using a test piece.

Individual tube mark-outs

One straight-blank diagram for every enabled tube

Each diagram uses one square-blank datum end (0 mm) and shows the full preparation-blank length. Bent members show start, centre and end of each developed bend. Straight members show the theoretical joint-centre/reference stations and end allowances instead. These reference stations are not finished fishmouth cut edges.

—

Diagrams are not 1:1 and are not a bend-machine feed program. Use the numeric millimetre dimensions, verify tube end orientation, test-bend CLR/springback, and check the separate labelled 1:1 fishmouth wrap-arounds. Cutting/prep blanks include allowances but do not account for the final fishmouth tip-to-tip profile.

Fabrication and safety check: This is an ideal geometric centreline layout, not a certified cage design, calibrated bend program or structural analysis. The brace, harness and side-bar dimensions exclude final notches and weld gaps. Harness anchorage and side-intrusion design must comply with relevant motorsport requirements; this calculator does not certify belt angles or cage safety. Confirm tube grade/size, bends, clearance, mounting and design against your applicable motorsport rules.
