1:1 pipe wrap-around templates

Print and wrap the unrolled mitre shapes around round pipe. The shapes are based on the segmented bend's outside diameter and mitre angle from square.

Printing: select 100% / Actual size, use the same paper size as above, disable Fit to page and browser headers/footers. Measure the 50 mm check bar on every sheet before using the pattern. If tiled, join sheets by matching the 25 mm registration grid, overlapping each tile by 10 mm. Use only the seam lines to set the pipe circumference.

The bold curves are cut edges; dashed lines are alignment guides. The seam is on the outside (long side) of the bend; the middle of the wrap is the inside (short side). The extra 8 mm strip is a taping tab, not extra circumference. For full pieces, cut out the template and trace both ends. For the reusable single-cut pattern, align the dashed square reference line to a square end: the shortest cut point is 35 mm beyond that line. Trace the curved edge and flip the pattern end-for-end for an opposite-facing cut. These are geometric layouts: allow separately for saw kerf, weld gap, bevel, fit-up and pipe tolerances.
