Steel cutting optimiser.

Nest cut lengths into mixed steel stock sizes, calculate kerf and clamp allowances, and visualise the cutting layout. Everything runs locally in your browser.

1. Set up the job

?Choose the type of steel section. This filters the size options; it does not change cut lengths or stock automatically.
?Pick a sample Australian section designation. These are convenient starting points, not a guarantee of supplier availability. Select Custom size to enter another size.
?Enter your actual profile designation. This will be used in the exported CSV and checked against any profile column in the imported cut list.
Illustrative Australian section presets. Always verify section dimensions, grade and availability with your supplier.
?List stock lengths in millimetres, such as 12000, 8000. Prefix a quantity with x, such as 4x12m. Stock without a quantity is treated as unlimited; shorter offcuts may also be entered.
Unlimited: 12000, 8000. Limited stock: 4x12m, 10x8m, 1x3500. You can include reusable offcuts.
?Material lost in the cut. This planner counts one kerf between adjacent parts on a bar, not at the free end. Example: 3 mm for a bandsaw, or 10 mm for RoboRail. Use the actual kerf for your machine.
?Length that the machine clamp or chuck cannot cut on each stock bar. It reduces usable stock length. Set 0 mm when no additional clamp allowance is required. For example, 400 mm reserved from a 12,000 mm bar leaves 11,600 mm usable.
Offcuts
?Tick Keep offcuts to label all positive leftover usable lengths as KEEP; untick to mark them SCRAP. This changes only the offcut labels and CSV output, not cut placement or bar quantities.
?Enter one item per line as Part mark, length in mm, quantity. Example A01,3500,2. You can also type 3500 x 2. Imported lists should contain only one profile at a time.
Format: Part, length mm, quantity per line, or 3500 x 2. One section size per run.
Excel XLS/XLSX, CSV/TSV and text-based steel material-list PDFs. Reads Mark / Profile / Grade / Qty / L(mm), including repeated headers. Files stay on your device. Maximum 8 MB; scanned PDFs aren't supported.

Review imported material

One profile and grade per plan. Section and grade are copied into your plan automatically. PDF page headers and subtotals are ignored; review the imported quantities before cutting.
🔒 Parts and CSV files remain on your device. No server processing or workshop database connection.

2. Nesting results

Longest-first placement; tightest fit into existing bars, then shortest suitable new stock. One kerf between adjacent parts. No unverified shared-mitre savings.

Visual cutting bars will appear here.

Scope. Adapted from the production-safe stock-length nesting approach in an archived Workshop Machine Part Search build. This first public preview does not create NC1/TAF machine programs, read your workshop database or verify geometric mitre sharing. Mitred members must be entered at their full required length. All output is a planning aid only: verify end allowances, part orientation, machine constraints and cut sequence before cutting. This is a heuristic, not guaranteed globally optimal.
